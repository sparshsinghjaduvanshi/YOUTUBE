To deliver a more immersive and intuitive viewing experience,
 the website should integrate a custom video player with gesture-based controls. 
 Users should be able to double-tap on the right side of the player to move the
  playback 10 seconds forward, and double-tap on the left side to move 10 seconds
   backward. A single tap in the center of the screen should pause or resume playback, 
   while three taps in the center should automatically skip to the next video. Additionally,
    three taps on the right side of the screen should close the website, and three taps on
     the left side should open the comment section. These gesture interactions create a 
     modern, app-like experience that enhances usability and user engagement.
