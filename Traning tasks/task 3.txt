To offer tiered access and encourage premium engagement, the platform should
 introduce a plan upgrade feature that allows users to move from the Free plan 
 to Bronze, Silver, or Gold. Users on the Free plan should be limited to watching
  videos for 5 minutes, while the Bronze plan (₹10) allows 7 minutes, the Silver 
  plan (₹50) allows 10 minutes, and the Gold plan (₹100) provides unlimited video 
  watching time. Once a user successfully completes the payment for any upgrade,
   the system must trigger an email notification containing the invoice and plan 
   details, ensuring transparency and confirmation of the transaction. This feature creates a
 clear value ladder while providing users with flexible viewing options.
