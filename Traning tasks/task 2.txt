To enhance user convenience and engagement, the platform should provide a video
 download feature that allows users to download videos directly from the website. 
 Downloaded videos should be accessible under a “Downloads” section in the user’s profile. 
 Free users should be allowed to download only one video per day. To download more than one 
 video per day, users must upgrade to the premium plan by clicking the premium option and 
 completing a payment via Razorpay (configured for test payments initially). Upon successful
payment, premium users should be able to download an unlimited number of videos, 
providing flexibility and incentivizing subscription upgrades.
